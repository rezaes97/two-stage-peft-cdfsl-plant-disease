01 / About

Research built around real-world constraints.

Many visual recognition problems are defined by two constraints: limited labeled examples and distribution shifts between development and deployment. I am interested in methods that address both without requiring expensive end-to-end retraining. This includes adapting pretrained visual models with lightweight modules, learning from a small support set, and developing representations that are useful beyond curated benchmarks.

03 / Publication

Selected work

A peer-reviewed study on data-efficient recognition in challenging in-field imagery.

Computers and Electronics in Agriculture 2026 · Research article · 111691

Dual-stage parameter-efficient tuning for in-field plant disease few-shot classification

Saeed Khankalantary and Mohammad Reza Eskandari

This work presents a two-stage adaptation pipeline for plant-disease recognition under data scarcity and domain shift. Offline domain adaptation uses lightweight adapters to learn domain-relevant visual priors; online few-shot adaptation then specializes the model to novel classes using a small labeled support set. The evaluation covers PlantDoc, PlantSeg, and PlantWild.
